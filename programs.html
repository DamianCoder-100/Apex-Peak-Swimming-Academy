Skill Development

Progress From Where You Are.

Swimming development is a process. We begin by understanding the swimmer's current ability and then work toward the skills that make sense for their next stage of development.

Find Your Starting Point
01

Water Familiarity

Become more comfortable in and around the water while learning basic safety and movement skills.

02

Foundation Skills

Develop essential skills such as floating, breathing, body position, kicking, and basic propulsion.

03

Stroke Development

Build stronger swimming technique and improve coordination, efficiency, and control in the water.

04

Continued Progress

Continue developing swimming ability through purposeful practice and increasingly challenging skills.
