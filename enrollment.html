The Assessment Process

Here's How It Works.

The assessment is designed to give us a better understanding of where you are and what you want to accomplish.

01

Tell Us About You

Share your swimming experience, goals, and anything you would like us to know.

02

Assess Your Skills

Your current comfort level and swimming skills help establish an appropriate starting point.

03

Identify Your Goals

We consider what you want to achieve and which areas you would like to improve.

04

Choose Your Path

We help identify the program and starting level that best fits your needs.

What to Expect

A Simple Way to Get Started.

You don't need to know your exact swimming level before booking. The purpose of the assessment is to help determine that.

  • Discuss your swimming experience and goals.
  • Review your current comfort level in the water.
  • Identify areas for development.
  • Determine an appropriate starting program.
  • Establish a path for continued progress.
